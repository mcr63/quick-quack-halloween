## Probably have already

- 3 servos, each with a 6-arm star horn and the screws from its bag. The bag screws hold the brush servos in their bases.
- 1 in PVC offcuts: 2 pieces, 36 in each, for the brush top-guide drop pipes.
- 4 wood screws, about 1¼ in, to mount the gate back plate.
- 5 V 6 A power brick and the ESP32.

## Only if needed

- **5 V 10 A power supply (~$20).** Get this if the brushes stall or the ESP32 resets while everything runs.
- **Push button (~$3).** For the Start Wash button. Touching the two wires together works for testing.
- **Gaffer tape.** Use it if the tubes feel loose on the cones.
- **A few small washers.** Use them as shims under the gate servo ears if the horn doesn't seat.
