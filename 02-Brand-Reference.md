# Brand Reference

Quick facts about Quick Quack Car Wash, taken from a research pass on Sept 22, 2026. Items marked **(unverified)** came from one weak source. Don't copy the logo art by hand. Use the logo SVG from the brand-assets chat (see below).

## Colors

Green, yellow, and orange, with white and black. The feel is playful, rounded, and cartoon-like.

| Color | Hex | Use | Source |

## Mascot

- Big yellow duck named **Quackals**, created in 1999 in Amarillo, TX.
- One Facebook post mentions "Wonky the duck." It's not clear if that's another name or another character. Stick with Quackals.
- **Quackenstein** is listed as a Halloween character on Grokipedia **(unverified)**.
- They also have a branded inflatable tube man.

## Uniform

- Duck-print neckties. This is well sourced and it's the key detail.
- Quick Quack sent us ties and visors. The attendants wear those.
- Shirt is not confirmed. We use green or yellow polos to match the logo.
- Staff check customers out on tablets at the pay stations.

## Tunnel elements

What a real site has, and what we copy.

| Real thing | Our version |
|---|---|
| Yellow signs and canopies | The 3 ft printed sign over the entrance, plus foam board signs |
| Pay station with tablet checkout | Cardboard podium, a tablet, and the printed servo gate arm |
| About 3-minute conveyor | Yellow tape lane on the floor |
| High-pressure pre-soak, QSOAP (pH-balanced soap) | Bubble machine. Real foam color isn't documented, so pick one. |
| "Flex Wrap" soft cloth strips and neoprene foam, no brushes | The Sonotube oscillating brush, plus tablecloth strips and pool noodles |
| Power-blow dryers | Box fans plus a blower sound |
| Tire shine, bug removal | Signs only |
| LED lighting | LED strip over the entrance |
| Free vacuum stalls near the exit | Shop vac with a "Free Vacuums" sign |
| About 99% of water reclaimed | Optional sign |

## Utah and local notes

- Lots of Utah County locations: Orem (3+), Provo, Lehi (3), Pleasant Grove, American Fork, Highland, Saratoga Springs, Springville, Spanish Fork (3), Mapleton, Payson, Santaquin.
- No Lindon location found. Nearest is likely **Orem, 1588 N State St**. Go there for reference photos of signs, the pay station, and uniforms.
- The company was founded in Sacramento in 2004 and has about 340-365 locations. It made Yelp's 50 Most Loved Brands 2025.

## Halloween precedents

- Some locations run a family-friendly haunted car wash in late October: costumed staff greeting cars, fog, black lights, and joke signs about "adult frights" like rent and student loans. Worth checking if Orem is doing one this year.
- A Quick Quack parade float is on YouTube.
- A "Duck Wash" mascot suit has been photographed in Salt Lake County.
- Quick Quack's own Facebook has posted car-wash DIY costume ideas.
- TikTok has duck dancing and employee waving content. Good for the mascot's moves.
