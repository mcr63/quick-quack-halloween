## WRONG WAY sign

Oct 28: orange sign with yellow WRONG WAY (32.5 mm capitals, 0.8 mm black outline) and Matt's rear-view duck (105 mm tall) walking off on the right. No plaque. Clips onto 1 in PVC: 4 receivers glue to the back in a plus pattern, and 2 snap clips push into them. Top and bottom receivers for a vertical pole, left and right for a horizontal crossbar on the car wash frame. For a free-standing pole, print a second HONK floor base. AMS slots: 1 yellow, 2 orange, 3 white, 4 black. Native Bambu files, both sliced cleanly in Bambu Studio 02.06.00.51. One plate by object isn't possible: the 20 mm clips need about 72 mm of toolhead clearance from the sign, and that doesn't fit on the bed. A paper version (tabloid and letter) is on the "Wrong Way Sign" design canvas.
